Model assumptions and limitations
  • The NMOS uses the long-channel square-law model: ID = β[(VGS − VTH)VDS − VDS²/2] in triode and ID = β(VGS − VTH)²/2 in saturation. Current is zero below threshold.
  • Channel-length modulation, body effect, gate current, device capacitances, temperature, and noise are omitted. The model is educational and does not replace a SPICE model or measured device.
  • Unspecified values are assumed for C1 = 10 µF, C2 = 10 µF, CS = 100 µF, signal-source resistance Rsig = 600 Ω, and output load RL = 1 MΩ. Coupling capacitors are open for the DC bias calculation.
  • The waveform solver numerically integrates the C1, CS, and C2 networks and solves the nonlinear drain/source operating equations at every step. The frequency-response plot is a linearized small-signal estimate about the Q-point and assumes saturation with negligible output conductance.
  • At large input levels, the waveform can clip or enter cutoff/triode operation. The small-signal gain and frequency response do not predict that distortion.
